# When Then?

A party game played in person, each player on their own phone, where players place historical statements on a growing personal timeline by guessing when they happened.

## Language

### Cards and timelines

**Card**:
A single historical statement together with when it happened and its category. It is the thing read aloud and placed on a timeline. How precisely its date is known varies (decade, year, month or day), but a card from 1500 onwards is always known at least to the year; the game mode decides how precisely it must be placed.
_Avoid_: Event, statement, fact, question

**Difficulty**:
How obscure the knowledge on a card is, from 1 (most people know it) to 4.
_Avoid_: Obscurity

**Theme**:
A set of cards on one subject, such as Sports or Music, that a game can be played with, like an expansion to a board game. Unrelated to a card's category, which is the type of card and what the category draw picks.
_Avoid_: Pack, theme pack, category

**Draft / Published / Archived**:
The life of a card: a draft is never dealt, a published card can be dealt in new games, and an archived card is kept but not dealt. Drafts and published cards can both be archived, and unarchiving returns a card to what it was. A card can only be published with its English text and a date of at least a decade (a year from 1500 onwards). Categories can be archived too, taking them out of the category draw for new games.
_Avoid_: Retired, deleted, inactive

**Anchor year**:
A random year given to each player at the start of a game, sitting on their timeline as the first reference point. It is not a card and is worth no points.
_Avoid_: Hook, starting year, base year

**Timeline**:
A player's chronologically ordered sequence of their anchor year and the cards they have placed.

**Unlocked**:
A card correctly placed during the current turn that will be lost if the player misplaces a later card in the same turn.
_Avoid_: At stake, pending

**Locked**:
A card that is permanently on a player's timeline and can no longer be lost.
_Avoid_: Banked, secured

**Lock in**:
To end your turn voluntarily, turning all unlocked cards into locked cards.
_Avoid_: Bank, pass

### Turns and rounds

**Turn**:
One player's sequence of plays, ending when they lock in or misplace a card.

**Play**:
One card dealt to the active player and their attempt to place it on their timeline. Lock in is offered only after a correct play; choosing to continue starts a new play within the same turn.
_Avoid_: Placement, action, guess, move

**Round**:
The span in which every player has had one turn.

**Category draw**:
The random selection before each card is dealt that decides which category the next card is dealt from: either a specific category, Reader's choice, or Free choice.
_Avoid_: Category roll, category die, spin

**Reader's choice**:
A category draw outcome where the reader picks the category.

**Free choice**:
A category draw outcome where the active player picks the category.
_Avoid_: Your choice, player's choice

**Dealt**:
How a card reaches the reader for a turn. Cards are dealt, never drawn; "draw" is reserved for the category draw.
_Avoid_: Drawn

**Steal**:
An attempt by another player to place a card the active player misplaced. The card passes around the table in seating order, ending with the reader, until someone places it correctly; a card nobody places is discarded for the rest of the game. A successful steal locks the card on the stealer's timeline and does not affect turn order.
_Avoid_: Pass, rebound, hand-over

**Reader**:
The player seated before the active player, who reads the card aloud.
_Avoid_: Asker, quizmaster

**Host**:
The player who created the game. The host also plays, and is the only one who can change the game's settings, start the game, skip or remove an absent player, end the game early and start a rematch.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner (an admin manages cards and categories, unrelated to playing)

### Starting and ending a game

**Game code**:
The four letters that identify a game, used to join it, until the game finishes.
_Avoid_: PIN, room code, game ID

**Lobby**:
The stage of a game before it starts, where players join, the host adjusts the settings, and anyone but the host can still leave. Starting the game closes it to new players.
_Avoid_: Waiting room

**Seating**:
The order the players sit in around the table, clockwise, which is also the turn order. The game waits in seating while the host arranges it: once before play starts, and between rounds when the host has asked to reseat.
_Avoid_: Seat order, table order

**Confirm seating**:
The host fixing the seating so play can start or resume. The first time, it gives every player their anchor year and picks a random starting player. A two-player game skips seating.

**Absent**:
A player whose phone has lost contact with the game. They keep their seat and can return to it.
_Avoid_: Away, offline, disconnected

**Skip**:
The host moving play past whatever an absent player is holding up: their turn, their steal attempt or their reading. The player keeps their seat and score.

**Remove**:
The host taking a player out of a game for good, together with their timeline. A removed player cannot win or return to their seat. During the lobby, a removed player can join again with the code.
_Avoid_: Kick

**Abandoned**:
A game that ended through inactivity rather than by being won or ended by the host. It has no winner.

**Rematch**:
A new game started from a finished one, beginning in a lobby with the same players, seating and settings, all of which can still change before it starts.
_Avoid_: Replay, new round

**Target score**:
The number of points a player must reach to win, from 3 to 30 (10 unless the host changes it in the lobby), fixed once the game starts.
_Avoid_: Winning score

**Game mode**:
The rules for how precisely cards must be placed, chosen when the game is created. The standard mode places cards from 1500 onwards by year and earlier cards by decade; a card not known that precisely is not dealt. A game mode changes what an answer is, unlike a house rule, which tweaks scoring or locking.
_Avoid_: Difficulty level

**Exact-year house rule**:
A house rule, off unless the host turns it on in the lobby, that rewards knowing a card's exact year rather than only where it goes on the timeline. How it plays is still open (#20).

**Card language**:
The language a game's cards are read aloud in, independent of each player's own app language. It starts as the host's app language, the host can change it in the lobby, and it is fixed once the game starts. A card without text in that language is read in English.

### Authoring

**Editor**:
A person who writes, translates, publishes and archives cards.
_Avoid_: Author, contributor, moderator

**Admin**:
An editor who also manages categories, who has access, card imports and the full history of changes. Unrelated to the host of a game.
_Avoid_: Host, superuser

**Machine / Reviewed / Stale translation**:
The state of a card's or category's text in a language other than English. A machine translation was produced by AI and not yet checked; a reviewed translation has been read by an editor; a stale translation was reviewed before the English text changed. All three can be played.
_Avoid_: Draft translation, unverified (verification is about a card's date, not its wording)
