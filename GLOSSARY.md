# When Then?

A party game played in person, each player on their own phone, where players place historical statements on a growing personal timeline by guessing when they happened.

## Language

### Cards and timelines

**Card**:
A single historical statement together with the year it happened, its category and its language. It is the thing read aloud and placed on a timeline.
_Avoid_: Event, statement, fact, question

**Anchor year**:
A random year given to each player at the start of a game, sitting on their timeline as the first reference point. It is not a card and is worth no points.
_Avoid_: Hook, starting year, base year

**Timeline**:
A player's chronologically ordered sequence of their anchor year and the cards they have placed.

**Unlocked**:
A card correctly placed during the current turn that will be lost if the player misplaces a later card in the same turn.
_Avoid_: At stake, pending

**Locked**:
A card that is permanently on a player's timeline and can no longer be lost.
_Avoid_: Banked, secured

**Lock in**:
To end your turn voluntarily, turning all unlocked cards into locked cards.
_Avoid_: Bank, pass

### Play

**Turn**:
One player's sequence of placements, ending when they lock in or misplace a card.

**Round**:
The span in which every player has had one turn.

**Category draw**:
The random selection at the start of each turn that decides which category the next card is dealt from: either a specific category, Reader's choice, or Free choice.
_Avoid_: Category roll, category die, spin

**Reader's choice**:
A category draw outcome where the reader picks the category.

**Free choice**:
A category draw outcome where the active player picks the category.
_Avoid_: Your choice, player's choice

**Dealt**:
How a card reaches the reader for a turn. Cards are dealt, never drawn; "draw" is reserved for the category draw.
_Avoid_: Drawn

**Steal**:
An attempt by another player to place a card the active player misplaced. The card passes around the table in seating order until someone places it correctly; a successful steal locks the card on the stealer's timeline and does not affect turn order.
_Avoid_: Pass, rebound, hand-over

**Reader**:
The player seated before the active player, who reads the card aloud.
_Avoid_: Asker, quizmaster

**Target score**:
The number of points a player must reach to win, set when the game is created.
_Avoid_: Winning score
